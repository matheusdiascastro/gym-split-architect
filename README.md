# 🏋️ IronBlueprints — Adaptive Training Protocol Architect

> **A precision-crafted companion for lifters, coaches, and movement enthusiasts who want their training split to evolve alongside their ambitions.**

IronBlueprints is a next-generation training protocol architect inspired by the timeless elegance of classic split methodologies — Push/Pull/Legs, Upper/Lower/Ab, Full-Body-Workout progressions, and the noble Bro Split — yet engineered for the lifter of 2026 who demands data-informed adaptation, aesthetic clarity, and a workflow that respects their time under the bar.

Where traditional generators spit out a static spreadsheet and wave goodbye, IronBlueprints behaves more like a seasoned coach leaning on the squat rack: it listens, it adjusts, and it hands you a blueprint that fits the shape of your week, your recovery, and your goals.

## 🌱 Why IronBlueprints Exists

Every serious lifter eventually hits the same wall: **the program that built your first year of progress stops speaking your language by year three.** You outgrow angle-based prescriptions. Your recovery window shifts. Your schedule flexes unpredictably because life — jobs, commutes, family, travel — rearranges the days on the calendar you swore you'd protect.

IronBlueprints was born from that friction. It is a repository that treats the training split not as a rigid document but as a **living organism** — one that breathes with your weekly rhythm, mutates when fatigue accumulates, and self-corrects when progression stalls.

The name is a nod to the blueprints of old shipyards — meticulously drawn, revised, and stamped before a vessel ever touches water. Your training week deserves the same care.

---

## 🧭 Core Philosophy

1. **Structure liberates.** A well-shaped split removes decision fatigue. When the plan is clear, effort flows into execution rather than into planning.
2. **Adaptation beats dogma.** There is no universal best split. There is only the best split *for you, this month, this mesocycle.*
3. **Clarity over clutter.** Minimal interface noise. Maximum signal. Every screen answers a single question well.
4. **Respect for recovery.** Volume is a debt. IronBlueprints helps you audit it before the interest compounds.
5. **Craft over hype.** No exaggerated promises. No miracle transformations. Just disciplined, repeatable architecture.

## 🗂️ Supported Splits & Methodologies

IronBlueprints ships with a curated catalogue of classical and hybrid splits. Each is treated as a first-class template capable of being reshaped, not merely selected.

- **Push / Pull / Legs** — the perennial triad, expanded into 3, 4, 5, and 6-day rotations with recovery-aware placement.
- **Upper / Lower / Ab** — a three-pillar arrangement balancing pressing, pulling, and trunk integrity.
- **Upper / Lower** — the four-day standard for lifters who want frequency without drowning in sessions.
- **Full-Body-Workout (FBW) / Ab** — dense, efficient, and ideal for time-boxed weeks.
- **FBW Beginner Path** — a gentle on-ramp that prioritizes motor pattern acquisition over load escalation.
- **Bro Split** — the culturally beloved body-part-per-day template, honored here with modern recovery accounting.

Each split can be layered with modifiers: **strength bias**, **hypertrophy bias**, **conditioning sprinkles**, **deload scheduling**, and **travel-week compression**.

## 🎨 How It Feels To Use

Imagine opening IronBlueprints on a Sunday evening. Instead of staring at a blank page, you see a canvas already shaped to your declared preferences: six sessions, two of them flagged as heavy, one as a deload, and a note reminding you that Thursday's schedule historically becomes chaotic.

You nudge Tuesday's pressing volume upward. The ledger updates in real time. The Recovery Sentinel whispers that your lower-back load is trending high — not to block you, but to make the trade visible. You accept, adjust, and close the laptop.

The next morning the plan is waiting, calm and legible, on your phone.

That is the entire point: **the tool should feel like a well-organized training journal that has already done the thinking, so you can spend your attention where it belongs — under tension.**

## 🏗️ Architecture Overview

IronBlueprints is organized around a small number of deeply considered modules:

1. **Blueprint Core** — the schema describing splits, sessions, and movement slots.
2. **Adaptation Layer** — rules governing how a blueprint mutates between mesocycles.
3. **Ledger Service** — the accounting sub-system for volume, intensity, and recovery.
4. **Presentation Shell** — the responsive, multilingual user surface.
5. **Concierge Bridge** — the always-available support channel integration.

Each module communicates through a narrow, documented contract, which keeps the repository approachable for newcomers and resilient for maintainers.

## 🌐 Multilingual Support

Language should never be a barrier to a good training week. IronBlueprints is built with internationalization at its foundation rather than as an afterthought. Copy is externalized, directionality is respected, and number/date formatting follows regional convention.

Currently anticipated locales include English, Polish, German, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian, and several Nordic variants — with the catalogue designed to accept new translations without touching core logic.

## 📱 Responsive Experience

The layout is not merely *tolerant* of small screens; it is *designed* for them. Rep counters should be tappable with chalk-dusted fingers. Session cards should be readable in a glance between sets. Menus should not require a magnifying glass.

Simultaneously, the desktop presentation embraces the extra canvas — side-by-side comparison of two candidate splits, a full-month ledger view, and a blueprint timeline that finally has room to breathe.

## 🛎️ Concierge & Assistance

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Is IronBlueprints a replacement for a real coach?**
No. It is an architect's drafting table, not the architect. It organizes and informs; it does not diagnose or prescribe medically.

**Do I need special equipment?**
The blueprints can be shaped around whatever a lifter has access to — a full commercial gym, a home rack, or a pair of adjustable implements.

**Can I bring my own split?**
Yes. The composer accepts hand-authored blueprints and treats them as first-class citizens.

**Will my data be shared with anyone?**
The design embraces an offline-first posture. You decide what leaves your device.
